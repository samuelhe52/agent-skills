# Paper Summarization

Create two complementary artifacts from the paper or report:

- **`reading-guide.md`**: A two-level outline following the source's sections and immediate subsections, with a short summary paragraph for each item.
- **`analytical-synthesis.md`**: An independent organization of the material that explains its central ideas, how they fit together, and what the reader should take away. Choose the structure and depth to suit the source and the user's interests. Give more attention to what matters; connect ideas across sections and assess the support for consequential claims where useful. Keep the authors' claims distinguishable from your interpretation.

Avoid imposing a fixed analytical template: an empirical paper, a theoretical argument, a survey, and an engineering report call for different treatments. Include source pointers where they help the reader return to important passages or evidence.

Use the user's requested location and names, or established project conventions. Otherwise, save both files under `paper-summaries/<paper-slug>/` in the current working directory. Identify and link the source in each artifact, and link both completed files in the handoff.
